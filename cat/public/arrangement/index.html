What this course is

DDA1000 is a structured induction programme for newly arrived SDS students.

Through classroom meetings, invited talks, alumni and faculty sharing, group discussion, and self-reflection, you will be guided to set goals and plan for study in the School of Data Science and for university life toward the upper years. Topics span data science, AI, and their social impact.

About 500 students are divided into 14 sessions. Each session is led by a professor (course instructor and academic advisor), sometimes with a co-advisor, and supported by 2–3 upper-year peer advisors / course assistants.

Course goals

What we hope you will gain this term.

  1. 01Welcome SDS freshmen and support a smooth first-year transition.
  2. 02Explain what university education means and what university life is like.
  3. 03Introduce data science and AI, and how SDS courses support study in these areas.
  4. 04Help you identify interests, set goals, and draft an initial plan for academic and personal development.
  5. 05Build a positive attitude for overcoming challenges in university study.
  6. 06Strengthen the SDS community and encourage communication among students and with SDS faculty.

Course journal

One journal per student — a record of your thoughts, reflections, views, and feelings across the course and your first term.

  • First submission: 30 October 2026 (returned to you on 6 November)
  • Final submission: 11 December 2026
  • SDS will keep your journal for four years and return it to you at graduation.
